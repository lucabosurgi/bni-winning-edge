Backlinks — why one line at the bottom of your website matters

When somebody in Spring Hill Googles “roofer” or “plumber”, Google has to decide who shows up first. A big part of how it decides: how many other real websites link to yours. Those links have a name — backlinks — and in SEO they're the thing that moves the needle most. Every backlink is a vote. You can't buy them honestly. You can't fake them. Most small businesses have almost none.

Now count what's in our room. About fifty local businesses. About fifty real websites. People who already trust each other. That's the gold. Not the directory — us.
